About NuVation

Practical business software for the people doing the work.

NuVation Business Solutions is building one connected workspace for the everyday records, jobs, deliveries, stock and team activity small businesses already manage. It is designed to work across PCs, laptops, tablets and phones, so teams can use the workspace where their work happens.

Why we built the ERP

For a plumber, builder, gardener, delivery team or local seller, the real work rarely happens in one place. Customer information, stock, jobs, invoices and vehicle activity can easily become separate files or disconnected tools. NuVation brings those records together while allowing a business to add only the operational modules it needs.

The product is designed around clear workflows, modular access and records that retain their relationships—from a supplier receipt to inventory cost, or from an invoice payment to bookkeeping income.

Our product principle

Every module must work with the base ERP independently and become more useful when compatible modules are also installed.

Built by a small business for small businesses

Meet the founder

NuVation Business Solutions is a South African sole proprietorship operated by Jaco du Toit in Potchefstroom. After completing an NQF Level 5 qualification in Business Management and experiencing the realities of running a very small business, Jaco built NuVation around the connected record-keeping and operational tools he believed smaller businesses and sole proprietors would value.

This background informs the product design; it is not a claim that NuVation provides professional accounting, tax, legal, audit or security services.

Built for real teams

Different access for different responsibilities

Owner

Controls the business workspace, subscription, settings, team and Professional access.

Administrator

Supports business operations without receiving ownership or billing control.

Employee

Sees only the modules and actions selected for their role.

Driver

Sees assigned deliveries and the related vehicle-check workflow without access to ownership, billing or the wider workspace.

Professional

Uses a separate read-only financial workspace across invited businesses.

Clear boundaries

A tool for records—not a replacement for professionals

NuVation helps capture, organise, connect, search and print information supplied by users. It does not verify source documents or provide legal, tax, audit, assurance or professional accounting services.
