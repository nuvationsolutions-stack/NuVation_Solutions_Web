Optional operations

Add the work your business actually does.

Every module works with the base ERP on its own. Compatible connections help when you use both, but no optional module requires another one.

Deliveries — R100

Schedule and track invoice-linked customer deliveries. Drivers see only their assigned delivery journey.

Fleet & Maintenance — R100

Keep vehicles, machines and equipment, maintenance records, costs and source references together. Archive retired resources while retaining required history.

Calendar & Bookings — R100

Organise appointments, meetings and bookings without adding a separate scheduling tool.

Asset Register — R100

Record asset values, locations, status and disposal information.

Jobs & Job Cards — R100

Assign work and capture employee Job Cards for the work completed.

Project Manager — R150

Projects, linked Jobs and Job Cards together. It includes Jobs and replaces the standalone Jobs charge.

Advanced Analytics — R100

Read-only current-data insight across the active modules for the account.
