9. What the Service does

The Service helps authorised users organise customer and supplier details, inventory and purchase-cost layers, quotes, invoices, payments, receivables, bookkeeping records, deliveries, reusable vehicles/machines/equipment and maintenance, appointments, assets, jobs, job cards, projects and current-data analytics. Available functionality depends on the account's plan and permissions.

Jobs preserve separate accountability for the owner or employees assigned to the job and the person who records each completed Job Card. An owner may assign work to themself and complete the Job Card personally, including where the business has no employees. Where Fleet & Maintenance is active, reusable vehicles, machines or equipment may be linked to work without deducting inventory or creating an exclusive reservation. Availability and overlap warnings are advisory; the business remains responsible for real-world condition, availability and scheduling.

Automated links—such as converting a quote to an invoice, receiving stock, applying FIFO purchase costs, recording a payment, creating related bookkeeping entries or completing paid maintenance—are administrative conveniences. Receiving a supplier order can increase or create inventory and generate a related expense. Recording an invoice payment can generate related bookkeeping income. Completing paid fleet maintenance can generate a related maintenance expense. Users must inspect the resulting records and source documents.

Advanced Metrics & Analytics reads the account's current recorded data and displays only insights supported by the account's subscribed modules and the user's access. Estimates such as target contribution quantities, stock cover, sales movement and maintenance comparisons are management indicators, not forecasts, valuations or accounting profit.
