7. How to request access

  1. Identify the requested record with enough detail for NuVation to locate it.
  2. Explain the right that the record is required to exercise or protect and why the record is required for that purpose.
  3. Provide adequate proof of identity and, where acting for another person or body, proof of authority.
  4. State the preferred form of access and a reliable contact method.
  5. Submit the current prescribed Form 2 or a substantially similar complete request to the Information Officer.
  6. Pay a prescribed request, search, preparation or reproduction fee only if lawfully requested after written notice.

NuVation will process a valid request within the period required by PAIA, subject to any extension permitted by PAIA. A request does not automatically entitle a requester to another business's ERP records. NuVation may need to consult affected third parties, separate exempt material, redact protected information, refer the requester to the responsible customer business or refuse access on a lawful PAIA ground.

8. Decisions, fees and remedies

A decision will grant, partially grant or refuse access and will provide the information required by PAIA. Where fees are permitted, NuVation will use the current prescribed fee schedule and give the required written notice before charging a request-related fee.

PAIA does not provide an internal appeal against the head of a private body's decision. A requester may use the complaint process of the Information Regulator or approach a competent court, subject to the requirements and time periods in applicable law. Current complaint forms and instructions are available from the Regulator's PAIA page.

9. Processing of personal information

NuVation's Privacy Policy provides the detailed public notice. In summary, NuVation processes personal information to operate the public website and ERP, manage accounts and authorised access, provide requested workflows, support prepaid access and payment verification when enabled, respond to requests, protect the Service and meet legal obligations.

9.1 Categories of data subjects and information

  • Website visitors and enquirers: communication details, message content and limited hosting/request information made available by the relevant service.
  • Account holders and invited users: name or display name, email, account identifiers, profile type, membership, role, permissions, status and acceptance evidence.
  • Business owners and customers: business identity, contact, address, subscription, order, payment-reference and support information.
  • People recorded by a customer business: customer, supplier, employee, Professional, payer, driver, contact and other operational information selected and entered by that business.
  • Service providers and advisers: contact, contract, support, billing and professional information.

9.2 Recipients or categories of recipients

  • users and Professionals authorised by the relevant business account;
  • hosting, authentication, database, storage, email, security and payment providers used for the stated service purpose;
  • professional advisers and support providers subject to appropriate duties;
  • competent regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies or other recipients where disclosure is lawfully required; and
  • a lawful successor to the business, subject to applicable safeguards and notice.

9.3 Processing outside South Africa

Some website, cloud, authentication, storage, email or support providers may process information outside South Africa. NuVation will permit a cross-border transfer only where a ground in section 72 of POPIA applies, including adequate protection under law or binding agreement, valid consent where appropriate, or a transfer necessary for an applicable contract. Current provider categories and material processing changes are described in the Privacy Policy.

9.4 General security measures

NuVation uses risk-based technical and organisational measures intended to protect confidentiality, integrity and availability. Depending on the relevant service, measures include authenticated access, role and permission controls, tenant separation, restricted server operations, input validation, rate limiting, security-event records, provider access controls, testing and an incident-response process. No security measure or online service eliminates all risk.

10. Customer-controlled ERP records

A customer business generally determines why and how its operational records are processed and is generally the responsible party for that information. NuVation generally acts as its operator. A PAIA or POPIA request about those records may need to be directed to the customer business. NuVation will not disclose another business's records merely because NuVation technically hosts or processes them.
