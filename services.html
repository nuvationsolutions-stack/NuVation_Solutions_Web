Created-per-month allowances

A creation uses the allowance once. Deleting that record later does not return the used allowance. During an active multi-month prepaid term, the allowance resets at the start of each monthly usage window; unused allowance does not roll over.

Currently-stored limits

These measure how many eligible records exist now. A permitted permanent deletion can free a stored-record space. Updates do not consume another space.

Controlled independently

The business can select increases only for the activities it expects to use. Module-specific increases are available only with the related active module, and server-enforced maximums still apply.

Prices are stated per prepaid month in South African rand. At customer launch, owners will be able to choose 1, 3, 6 or 12 months for the base plan. Add-ons, seats and capacity may use an eligible shorter term or match the exact remaining base-plan period, but can never extend beyond the base expiry. The future checkout will show all products, dates, calculations and the total payable before confirmation. Nothing renews or charges automatically. Access will activate only after NuVation verifies a cleared payment through PayFast. Registration and checkout are not currently available.
