More than separate features

See the work, the people and the record behind it.

NuVation is designed so an owner can follow a job, delivery or sale from the first customer conversation to the final business record—without giving every team member access to everything.

Put the right person on the work

Owners invite administrators, employees, delivery drivers and a read-only trusted Professional separately. Employees receive only the module actions the owner enables. Delivery drivers see only their assigned operational work; the owner and authorised staff retain the wider delivery schedule.

Keep field work accountable

Assign Jobs to the owner or an employee, start work, pause when the team is interrupted, resume later and record an honest active-work total that excludes paused time. Job Cards keep the work completed, person and linked project together.

Deliver with proof and vehicle history

Schedule invoice-linked deliveries, assign a driver and vehicle, capture the recipient’s touch signature and retain delivery timing. Daily K53-style checks, opening and closing odometers, distance and owner-only shift corrections make vehicle activity easier to review.

Correct safely instead of losing history

Ordinary records can be edited or removed only where their relationships allow it. Connected, completed, paid or reviewed records use the appropriate controlled action—such as cancel, refund, correction or review—so important history is not silently overwritten.

A practical example

From a monthly garden visit to an accurate customer record

Create the customer and a project, add recurring or individual Jobs, assign the responsible person, record the Job Card and active time, then invoice the work. The owner can review completed visits for that customer or project before sending the invoice.

  1. 1
    Plan the work

    Customer, project, job, schedule and responsible person stay connected.

  2. 2
    Do and record it

    The assigned person records the real work and active time; a pause does not inflate it.

  3. 3
    Turn it into a business record

    Review the work, issue the invoice, record payment and keep the related records easy to trace.
