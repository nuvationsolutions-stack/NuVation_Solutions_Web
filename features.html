C

Customers

Keep contact, address, VAT and CIPC details ready for connected documents and customer history.

S

Supplier Manager

Manage suppliers, purchase orders, expected deliveries and received stock with linked expense evidence.

I

Inventory

Track products, services, stock levels, selling prices and recorded purchase-cost layers.

Q

Invoicing

Create quotes and invoices, choose VAT treatment, convert quotes, and open printable PDFs.

R

Receivables

Record full or partial payments, open receipts and prepare a current customer statement.

B
