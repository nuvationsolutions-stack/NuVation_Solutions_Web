The NuVation approach

Capture the record once. Keep the story connected.

Instead of copying details between separate lists, NuVation links everyday records so you can see what happened and where it belongs.

The core

Six modules cover the essential business record.

Customers, suppliers, inventory, invoices, payments and bookkeeping are designed to work together while remaining useful independently.

1. Receive stock

Keep the supplier purchase and cost information available.

2. Invoice work

Use connected customer and product information.

3. Record payment

Keep invoice balances current.

4. Keep the link

Generated bookkeeping records retain their source context.

5. Add operations

Activate work, fleet, delivery, booking or asset tools when needed.

6. Give clear access

Assign people the roles and modules they need.
