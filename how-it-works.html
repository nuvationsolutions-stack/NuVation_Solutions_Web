The NuVation approach

Capture the record once. Keep the story connected.

The base ERP connects the everyday records a business needs without requiring any optional operational module.

Included in the base ERP

Six modules cover the essential business record.

These six modules are included together in the NuVation base plan and are useful independently as well as when connected.

1. Customers

Keep customer contact, address, VAT and CIPC details ready for connected documents.

2. Supplier Manager

Keep suppliers, purchase orders, received stock and related cost information organised.

3. Inventory

Track products, services, stock levels, selling prices and recorded purchase-cost layers.

4. Invoicing

Create quotes and invoices using connected customer and product information.

5. Receivables Tracker

Record payments, keep invoice balances current, and open receipts or customer statements.

6. Bookkeeping

Organise manual and generated income or expense records with their source context.

Optional only when useful

Operations are separate add-ons.

Deliveries, Fleet, Calendar, Assets, Jobs, Projects and Advanced Analytics are not part of the six core modules. Choose them only if your business needs them.
